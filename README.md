# Sree Draupathi Builders & Contractors

> **"WE BUILD WHAT YOU NEED"**

Official responsive corporate website and administrative dashboard portal for **Sree Draupathi Builders & Contractors** — premier turnkey civil construction contractors specializing in residential houses, luxury villas, commercial complexes, 3D elevations, and interior fitouts.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🏛️ Visitor Website (`index.html`)
- **Luxury Brand Identity**: Featuring official 3D Gold & Navy SD House Emblem.
- **⚡ Live Interactive Construction Cost Estimator**:
  - Slider from 500 to 8,000 sq.ft.
  - Floor selector (G to G+3).
  - Multi-tier packages (*Standard* @ ₹1,850/sq.ft, *Premium* @ ₹2,250/sq.ft, *Royal Luxury* @ ₹2,750/sq.ft).
  - Instant ₹ calculation, civil/MEP/finishing breakdown, and 1-click **Send Estimate to WhatsApp**.
- **Services Showcase**: Residential, Commercial, Architectural 3D Elevation, Renovation, Interior Design & Turnkey Contracting.
- **5-Step Work Roadmap**: Consultation &rarr; Design Approval &rarr; Legal Agreement &rarr; Live Execution (Daily WhatsApp Updates) &rarr; Key Handover.
- **Filterable Portfolio Gallery**: Filter by *All*, *Residential*, *Commercial*, and *Interior*.
- **Client Testimonials & FAQ Accordion**.
- **Multi-Channel Contact & Lead Capture**: Quick callback consultation form, main enquiry form, and floating WhatsApp support.

## 🚀 How to Enable GitHub Pages (Free Live Website Hosting)

To make your website live on the internet with a public link:

1. Open this repository on [GitHub](https://github.com/ganieraa-cmyk/sree-draupathi-builders).
2. Click on **Settings** (top right tab).
3. On the left sidebar, click on **Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment** &rarr; **Branch**:
   - Select `main` branch.
   - Folder: `/ (root)`.
   - Click **Save**.
5. Within 1–2 minutes, GitHub will publish your site at:
   `https://ganieraa-cmyk.github.io/sree-draupathi-builders/`
